    How I Work

    01

    Start with the business problem

    I clarify who is affected, what decision needs to improve, and how success should be measured before choosing a technical approach.

    02

    Build the simplest useful solution

    I connect data, domain context, and engineering constraints to deliver something practical rather than optimizing technology in isolation.

    03

    Design for continuity

    I value clear assumptions, reproducible workflows, observability, and documentation so that a solution remains understandable and maintainable after delivery.

    My interest in combining scientific research, data, and product thinking also grew through a Global Challenger project in the United States focused on vegan cosmetics.
